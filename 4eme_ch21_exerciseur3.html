📐 Formulaire

Périmètres

Carré (côté c)P = 4 × c
Rectangle (L × l)P = 2 × (L + l)
TriangleP = a + b + c
Cercle (rayon r)P = 2 × π × r

Aires

CarréA = c × c
RectangleA = L × l
TriangleA = (b × h) ÷ 2
DisqueA = π × r²
ParallélogrammeA = b × h

Volumes

Cube (arête a)V = a³
Pavé droitV = L × l × h
CylindreV = π × r² × h
PrismeV = Aire base × h
